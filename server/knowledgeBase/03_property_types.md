# Dubai Residential Property Types & Configurations

## 1. Apartments
- **Configurations:**
  - **Studio:** 380 - 550 sq.ft. Single combined living/sleeping area with kitchenette and separate bathroom. Ideal for single professionals and high-yield short-term rentals.
  - **1 BHK (1 Bedroom Hall Kitchen):** 650 - 1,000 sq.ft. Separate master bedroom, living room, powder room or full bathroom, balcony.
  - **2 BHK (2 Bedroom Hall Kitchen):** 1,100 - 1,700 sq.ft. Two en-suite bedrooms, guest powder room, spacious living/dining area, maid's room in premium developments.
  - **3 BHK & 4 BHK:** 1,800 - 3,500 sq.ft. Large family apartments with formal dining, closed or open show kitchen, maid's quarters with en-suite, laundry room, multiple balconies.
- **Amenities:** Shared swimming pools, state-of-the-art gymnasiums, sauna/steam facilities, concierge desk, 24/7 security, allocated basement parking spaces.

## 2. Villas
- **Characteristics:** Standalone, detached independent homes with private boundary walls, private gardens, and private swimming pools (or space to construct one).
- **Configurations:** Typically 3 to 7 bedrooms, spanning 3,500 sq.ft up to 25,000+ sq.ft for bespoke palatial mansions.
- **Features:** Private covered double/triple garages, driver's room, maid's room, private elevator in multi-story villas, rooftop entertainment terraces.
- **Key Enclaves:** Palm Jumeirah Fronds, Emirates Hills, Dubai Hills Vista, Jumeirah Islands, Arabian Ranches, Al Barari.

## 3. Townhouses
- **Characteristics:** Multi-level single-family homes attached to adjacent units on one or both sides (end-units vs middle-units).
- **Configurations:** Usually 3 to 4 bedrooms spanning 1,900 - 3,200 sq.ft across Ground + 1 or Ground + 2 floors.
- **Features:** Private compact rear garden, covered two-car carport, access to master community parks, shared swimming pools, and sports facilities.
- **Key Enclaves:** Dubai Hills Maple, Arabian Ranches Reem/Sun/Joy, Damac Hills 2, Tilal Al Ghaf, Town Square.

## 4. Penthouses
- **Characteristics:** Top-floor or entire-floor super-prime residences offering panoramic 360-degree skyline or ocean views.
- **Configurations:** 4 to 6 bedrooms, duplex or triplex layouts spanning 5,000 to 20,000+ sq.ft.
- **Features:** Private rooftop infinity pools, private elevator lobbies with biometric access, double-height ceilings (6-8 meters), bespoke marble and Italian designer finishes.
- **Key Enclaves:** Palm Jumeirah (One Palm, Atlantis The Royal), Downtown Dubai (Il Primo, Address), Dubai Marina (Le Reve).

## 5. Branded Residences & Hotel Apartments
- **Characteristics:** Residential properties managed in partnership with global hospitality and luxury brands (e.g., Four Seasons, Bvlgari, Armani, Address, Dorchester Collection).
- **Benefits:** Five-star hotel services on demand (valet, 24/7 in-residence dining, housekeeping, concierge), strong brand equity, higher rental yields, and superior capital appreciation.
