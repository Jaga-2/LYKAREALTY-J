# Dubai Property Buying & Selling Procedures

## Overview of the Secondary Market Purchase Process
Acquiring real estate in Dubai's secondary (resale) market is straightforward, tightly regulated by RERA, and typically completes within 15 to 30 calendar days for cash purchases, or 30 to 45 days if mortgage financing is involved.

## Step-by-Step Purchase Workflow

### Step 1: Property Selection & Due Diligence
- The buyer selects the property with a licensed RERA real estate broker.
- Broker verifies Title Deed via the official Dubai REST app to confirm ownership and ensure no blocking encumbrances or court freezes exist on the property.

### Step 2: Contract Signing (Unified RERA Forms)
- **Form A:** Broker-Seller Agreement authorized via Dubai REST.
- **Form B:** Broker-Buyer Agreement establishing buyer representation.
- **Form F (Contract F / MOU):** The legally binding Unified Contract of Sale between Buyer and Seller. Specifies purchase price, payment terms, completion date, and deposit terms.
- **Security Deposit:** Buyer deposits a 10% refundable security deposit cheque in the name of the seller, held safely in escrow by the licensed brokerage until title transfer.

### Step 3: Mortgage Valuation (If Mortgage Financed)
- If buyer uses mortgage financing, the lending bank instructs an independent surveyor to value the property.
- Bank issues the Final Offer Letter (FOL) upon successful valuation and underwrites the mortgage loan.

### Step 4: Developer No Objection Certificate (NOC)
- Seller applies for an official No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the master developer (e.g. Emaar, Nakheel, Meraas, Damac).
- The developer issues the NOC once all outstanding service charges, maintenance fees, and district cooling liabilities are fully cleared.
- NOC fee ranges between AED 500 and AED 5,000 depending on developer and standard vs express processing.

### Step 5: Transfer of Ownership at DLD Trustee Office
- Buyer, seller, and their licensed brokers attend an authorized Dubai Land Department (DLD) Real Estate Registration Trustee Office.
- **Payment Settlement:**
  - Manager's Cheque for remaining purchase price payable to seller (or settlement cheque to seller's existing mortgage bank).
  - 4% DLD transfer fee payable to Dubai Land Department.
  - AED 4,000 + 5% VAT Trustee Office administration fee.
  - 2% + 5% VAT brokerage commission to licensed agency.
- The Trustee Officer signs and executes the official digital transfer on DLD servers.
- **Issuance of Title Deed:** The new electronic Title Deed (with QR code and DLD seal) is issued immediately to the buyer within minutes.
