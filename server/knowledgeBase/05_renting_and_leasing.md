# Dubai Renting & Leasing Framework

## Tenancy Contracts & Legal Standards
Residential leasing in Dubai is governed by Law No. 26 of 2007 (and its amendment Law No. 33 of 2008) regulating the relationship between landlords and tenants.

## Essential Leasing Components

### 1. Unified Tenancy Contract & Ejari
- **Ejari (Arabic for "My Rent"):** Mandatory government system managed by DLD that legally registers all tenancy agreements in Dubai.
- An official Ejari certificate is legally required to connect water and electricity (DEWA), activate internet/telecom services, register residency visas for family members, and access the Rental Dispute Settlement Centre (RDSC).
- Ejari registration fee is approximately AED 220.

### 2. Payment Terms & Cheques
- Annual rent in Dubai is traditionally paid via post-dated UAE bank cheques (typically 1, 2, 4, or 6 cheques per year).
- Paying in 1 single cheque often grants the tenant negotiating leverage to secure a 3% to 7% reduction in the annual rental price.
- Direct digital rent payments via direct debit (UAEDDS) and online rental payment gateways are increasingly adopted across modern institutional landlord portfolios.

### 3. Upfront Tenant Costs & Security Deposits
- **Security Deposit:**
  - Unfurnished property: 5% of annual rent (refundable at lease end).
  - Furnished property: 10% of annual rent (refundable at lease end).
- **Agency Commission Fee:** Standard 5% of annual rent (+ 5% VAT) payable to the licensed leasing broker.
- **DEWA Utility Deposit:**
  - Apartment: AED 2,000 refundable deposit (+ AED 130 connection fee).
  - Villa: AED 4,000 refundable deposit (+ AED 330 connection fee).
- **Housing Fee (Dubai Municipality):** 5% of annual rent, split across 12 monthly DEWA utility bills for expatriate tenants.

### 4. RERA Rental Increase Calculator (Decree No. 43 of 2013)
Landlords cannot arbitrarily increase rent upon renewal. Rent increases are strictly capped by the official RERA Rental Index based on how current rent compares to average market rent for identical units in the same community:
- Rent is less than 10% below market average: **0% increase allowed**.
- Rent is 11% to 20% below market average: **Maximum 5% increase allowed**.
- Rent is 21% to 30% below market average: **Maximum 10% increase allowed**.
- Rent is 31% to 40% below market average: **Maximum 15% increase allowed**.
- Rent is more than 40% below market average: **Maximum 20% increase allowed**.

### 5. Landlord Eviction Notice Rules
Under Article 25 of Law No. 33 of 2008, a landlord may request eviction of a tenant upon lease expiry only under specific statutory grounds (personal use for first-degree relatives, demolition/comprehensive reconstruction, or sale of the property). The landlord MUST provide an official **12-month written notice sent via Notary Public or registered mail**.
