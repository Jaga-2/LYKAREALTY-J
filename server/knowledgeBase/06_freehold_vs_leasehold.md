# Freehold vs Leasehold Property Ownership in Dubai

## Legal Basis of Foreign Ownership
In 2002, the Government of Dubai issued the landmark Freehold Decree, later codified in Law No. 7 of 2006. This legislation opened Dubai's real estate market to international global capital by creating two distinct property ownership categories for foreign expatriates.

## Freehold Ownership (100% Absolute Ownership)
- **Definition:** The buyer owns both the real estate structure and the land beneath it indefinitely and in perpetuity.
- **Title Document:** DLD issues an unconditional Title Deed registered in the investor's name (individual, joint, or qualifying corporate holding company registered in approved free zones like DIFC, JAFZA, or ADGM).
- **Rights:**
  - Owner has total legal freedom to lease, occupy, renovate, bequeath in a will, or sell the property at any time.
  - Property passes directly to legal heirs under UAE inheritance laws or registered DIFC Wills.
  - Ownership does not expire and has no time limit.
- **Designated Freehold Communities Include:**
  - Dubai Marina, Palm Jumeirah, Downtown Dubai, Dubai Hills Estate, Arabian Ranches, Business Bay, JBR, JLT, DIFC, Emirates Hills, Jumeirah Village Circle (JVC), Bluewaters Island, Jumeirah Bay Island, Dubai Creek Harbour, Meydan / MBR City.

## Leasehold Ownership (Long-Term Usufruct)
- **Definition:** The purchaser acquires the exclusive right to occupy, use, and lease the property for a designated long-term period, typically up to 99 years (or 30 to 50 years).
- **Land Ownership:** The ultimate ownership of the underlying land remains with the original freeholder (often a government body or UAE national landlord).
- **Title Document:** DLD issues an Usufruct or Musataha registration certificate.
- **Rights:**
  - At the expiration of the lease term, the property and improvements revert back to the freeholder unless an extension or renewal is agreed upon.
- **Predominant Leasehold Areas:**
  - Traditional historical districts: Deira, Bur Dubai, parts of Al Barsha, Al Karama, and certain designated non-freehold areas.
