# Dubai Land Department (DLD) & RERA Regulatory Safeguards

## Regulatory Hierarchy
Dubai's real estate governance consists of two primary statutory bodies:
1. **Dubai Land Department (DLD):** The sovereign government entity responsible for property registration, land ownership records, title transfers, and real estate legislation in Dubai.
2. **Real Estate Regulatory Agency (RERA):** The executive regulatory arm of DLD that licenses and oversees real estate brokerages, real estate agents, property developers, escrow accounts, joint ownership management, and marketing advertisements.

## Key Regulatory Protections for Buyers & Investors

### 1. Mandatory Project Escrow Accounts (Law No. 8 of 2007)
- By law, all off-plan property developers in Dubai must open a dedicated project escrow account with an approved UAE commercial bank accredited by DLD.
- All buyer installment payments are deposited directly into this project-specific escrow account.
- Developers CANNOT withdraw funds arbitrarily; funds are released by DLD in stages strictly tied to verified engineering inspection construction milestones (e.g. 20% completion = release of foundation tranche).
- 5% of total project construction funds are retained in escrow for one full year after building handover to cover defect remediation.

### 2. Oqood Pre-Registration for Off-Plan Purchases
- When buying an off-plan property, DLD pre-registers the purchase on the **Oqood** system (Arabic for "Contracts").
- Oqood acts as an interim digital title deed protecting buyer ownership rights before physical completion and title deed issuance.

### 3. Trakheesi Permitted Advertising
- Every property advertisement in Dubai must carry an official RERA Trakheesi Permit QR code and permit number.
- Brokers cannot advertise properties without a signed Seller Form A and verified Title Deed. This virtually eliminates fake listings and unauthorized broker spam.

### 4. Broker Licensing & Escrow Commission Holding
- All practicing real estate agents must pass the certified RERA examination, undergo police criminal background clearance, and hold a valid electronic Broker Card.
- Security deposit cheques and reservation deposits cannot be cashed directly by brokers; they must be held safely in fiduciary trust.
