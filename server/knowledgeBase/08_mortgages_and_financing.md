# Dubai Mortgages, Bank Financing & Central Bank Regulations

## UAE Central Bank Mortgage Regulations
Mortgage lending in Dubai is regulated by the Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE) to protect financial system stability and prevent excessive speculative leverage. Both residents and non-resident foreign investors can secure mortgage financing from UAE commercial banks (e.g. Emirates NBD, FAB, ADCB, DIB, HSBC).

## Minimum Down Payment & Maximum Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratios

### 1. Expatriate Residents:
- **First Property (Value up to AED 5 Million):**
  - Minimum Down Payment: **20%**
  - Maximum Loan Amount (LTV): **80%**
- **First Property (Value above AED 5 Million):**
  - Minimum Down Payment: **30%**
  - Maximum Loan Amount (LTV): **70%**
- **Second / Subsequent Investment Property:**
  - Minimum Down Payment: **40%**
  - Maximum Loan Amount (LTV): **60%**
- **Off-Plan Property Mortgage:**
  - Minimum Down Payment: **50%**
  - Maximum Loan Amount (LTV): **50%**

### 2. UAE Nationals:
- First Property (Value up to AED 5 Million): Minimum Down Payment **15%** (85% LTV).
- First Property (Value above AED 5 Million): Minimum Down Payment **25%** (75% LTV).

### 3. Non-Resident Foreign Investors:
- UAE banks provide non-resident mortgages to international overseas buyers.
- Minimum Down Payment: Typically **40% to 50%** (50% to 60% LTV).
- Proof of foreign income, 6 months bank statements, and tax returns are required.

## Debt Burden Ratio (DBR) & Eligibility Limits
- Under CBUAE rules, an individual's total monthly debt obligations (mortgage repayment + credit cards + car loans + personal loans) cannot exceed **50% of verifiable monthly net income**.
- For credit cards, banks factor 5% of the total credit card limit as a monthly commitment regardless of whether the card balance is zero.
- Maximum loan tenure is **25 years** (up to age 65 for salaried expats, or age 70 for self-employed individuals).

## Mortgage Interest Rates & Loan Structures
- **Fixed-Rate Mortgages:** Fixed for 1, 2, 3, or 5 years (typically 3.99% - 4.85%), reverting to variable EIBOR (Emirates Interbank Offered Rate) + bank margin (1.5% - 2.0%).
- **Variable-Rate Mortgages:** 3-month or 6-month EIBOR + bank margin.
- **Islamic Financing (Ijara / Murabaha):** Sharia-compliant profit-rate structures.

## Pre-Approval Process
Buyers should obtain a bank Mortgage Pre-Approval (valid for 60 to 90 days) before signing Form F. This guarantees financing eligibility, establishes exact budget boundaries, and prevents forfeiture of the 10% contract deposit cheque.
