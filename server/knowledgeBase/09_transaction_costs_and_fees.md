# Dubai Real Estate Transaction Costs and Fees

## Overview
When acquiring property in Dubai, buyers and investors must budget for regulatory, administrative, and brokerage costs beyond the agreed purchase price. In the secondary (resale) market, aggregate purchasing fees typically range between **6% and 7%** of the property purchase price. For primary (off-plan) purchases directly from developers, transaction costs are often reduced (typically **4%** DLD fee, which developers frequently waive or discount by 50% to 100% during promotional launches).

---

## 1. Primary Government & Regulatory Fees

### Dubai Land Department (DLD) Transfer Fee
- **Standard Rate:** **4.0%** of the contract property price.
- **Admin / Title Deed Fee:** **AED 580** (issued upon successful transfer).
- **Payment Distribution:** Legally split 50/50 between buyer and seller unless contractually agreed otherwise; in standard Dubai market practice, the buyer pays the full 4% unless negotiated differently in Form F (MOU).
- **Payment Method:** Manager's Cheque drawn in favor of Dubai Land Department or approved e-payment via the DLD trustee portal.

### Registration Trustee Office Fees
Transfers must be finalized at an authorized DLD Registration Trustee office.
- **Properties valued at AED 500,000 and above:** **AED 4,000 + 5% VAT** (Total: **AED 4,200**).
- **Properties valued below AED 500,000:** **AED 2,000 + 5% VAT** (Total: **AED 2,100**).

---

## 2. Professional & Developer Fees

### Real Estate Agency Commission
- **Standard Brokerage Fee:** **2.0%** of the agreed sale price + 5% VAT (effective **2.1%**).
- **Payment Timing:** Payable upon successful execution of Form F and completion of property transfer at the Trustee office.
- **Regulatory Requirement:** Brokers must possess an active RERA Broker Card and operate under a licensed real estate brokerage holding an active ORN (Office Registration Number).

### Developer NOC (No Objection Certificate) Fee
Required in secondary sales to verify that all service charges and community fees are cleared and the developer consents to the title transfer.
- **Standard Cost:** **AED 1,000 to AED 5,000 + VAT** depending on master developer (e.g., Emaar, Nakheel, DAMAC, Sobha, Meraas).
- **Processing Time:** 2 to 7 working days.
- **Refundable Security Deposit:** Some developers require an additional temporary deposit (AED 5,000) during NOC processing, refunded once the new Title Deed is presented.

## 3. Mortgage-Related Transaction Costs (Financed Purchases)

For buyers securing financing through a UAE Central Bank licensed lending institution:

| Fee Component | Typical Cost | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DLD Mortgage Registration Fee** | **0.25%** of the loan amount + **AED 290** | Mandatory government registration of the bank's mortgage lien on the title deed. |
| **Bank Valuation Fee** | **AED 2,500 – AED 3,500 + 5% VAT** | Paid directly to the bank to inspect and appraise the property before final loan approval. |
| **Bank Mortgage Arrangement / Processing Fee** | **0.50% to 1.00%** of loan amount + VAT | Many lenders cap this fee or offer fee-waiver campaigns. |
| **Life & Property Insurance** | 0.3% - 0.5% per annum | Mandatory requirement by UAE banks for residential mortgages. |

## 4. Summary Cost Calculator (Example: AED 2,000,000 Cash vs Financed)

### Cash Purchase of AED 2,000,000 Residence:
- **DLD Transfer Fee (4%):** AED 80,000
- **DLD Admin Fee:** AED 580
- **Trustee Office Fee:** AED 4,200
- **Agency Commission (2% + VAT):** AED 42,000
- **Developer NOC:** ~AED 2,500
- **Estimated Total Ancillary Costs:** **AED 129,280 (~6.46% of purchase price)**
- **Total Acquisition Outlay:** **AED 2,129,280**

### Mortgaged Purchase of AED 2,000,000 Residence (80% LTV = AED 1,600,000 Loan):
- **Down Payment (20%):** AED 400,000
- **DLD Transfer Fee (4%):** AED 80,000
- **Trustee & Admin Fees:** AED 4,780
- **Agency Commission (2% + VAT):** AED 42,000
- **DLD Mortgage Registration (0.25% + AED 290):** AED 4,290
- **Bank Valuation Fee:** AED 3,150
- **Bank Arrangement Fee (0.5% + VAT):** AED 8,400
- **Developer NOC:** ~AED 2,500
- **Total Upfront Cash Required:** **AED 545,120 (~27.25% of purchase price)**
