# UAE Golden Visa – Real Estate Investor Requirements

## Overview
The United Arab Emirates offers a prestigious **10-Year Renewable Golden Visa** to real estate investors who purchase residential or commercial properties in Dubai's designated freehold zones. The Golden Visa provides self-sponsored long-term residency, full family sponsorship rights, and exceptional lifestyle freedom without the traditional requirement of an employment sponsor or company formation.

---

## 1. Core Eligibility Criteria (10-Year Real Estate Golden Visa)

| Requirement | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Minimum Property Value** | **AED 2,000,000** (AED 2 Million, equivalent to approximately USD $545,000 / EUR €505,000 / GBP £435,000). |
| **Asset Aggregation** | The AED 2,000,000 threshold may be satisfied through **a single property or an aggregated portfolio** of multiple properties under the applicant's name. |
| **Jurisdiction** | Properties must be located within designated **Freehold zones** (e.g., Downtown Dubai, Dubai Marina, Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Hills Estate, Business Bay, JVC). |
| **Asset State** | **Ready Properties:** Fully titled with an official DLD Title Deed.<br>**Off-Plan Properties:** Registered via DLD Oqood contract with an approved master developer. |
| **Mortgage Regulations** | Properties financed through a UAE Central Bank licensed bank are eligible, provided the investor has paid at least AED 2,000,000 in equity, or holds an official bank NOC/statement for Golden Visa issuance. |
| **Joint Ownership** | **Spouses:** A legally married couple can combine property shares to satisfy the AED 2M threshold with an attested marriage certificate.<br>**Unrelated Partners:** Each co-owner must individually own an equity share valued at AED 2,000,000 or more. |

---

## 2. 2-Year Investor Visa Alternative (AED 750,000+)

For buyers whose investment falls below AED 2,000,000 but meets or exceeds **AED 750,000**, the Dubai Land Department offers a **2-Year Renewable Property Investor Visa**:
- Requires completed ready residential property with Title Deed.
- If mortgaged, a minimum 50% paid equity or AED 375,000 paid to the bank is required.
- Renewable every 2 years as long as the property remains under the investor's ownership.

---

## 3. Key Golden Visa Privileges and Benefits

1. **Self-Sponsored Long-Term Residency:** 10-year residency with zero reliance on an employer, local partner, or corporate sponsor.
2. **Exemption from the 6-Month Absence Rule:** Standard UAE resident visas lapse if the holder remains outside the UAE for more than 180 continuous days. Golden Visa holders can stay outside the UAE for any duration without losing their residency status.
3. **Comprehensive Family Sponsorship:** The principal investor can sponsor their legal spouse and children of any age (unmarried daughters of any age and sons up to 25 or older if student/dependent).
4. **Domestic Staff Sponsorship:** Unlimited sponsorship of domestic helpers, drivers, and private staff.
5. **Family Continuity:** In the event of the primary visa holder’s demise, sponsored family members can retain their Golden Visa status until the end of its 10-year term.
6. **Esaad Privilege Discount Card:** Access to government institutional discounts across healthcare, hospitality, dining, automotive, and retail.

---

## 4. Application Process & Required Documents

The application is processed directly through the **DLD Cube Centre** or the **General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA / ICP)**:

### Required Documentation:
- Original Title Deed or electronic DLD Title Deed certificate.
- Valid Passport copy (minimum 6 months validity).
- Current UAE visit visa, entry permit, or residence visa cancellation.
- UAE Medical Fitness test result from an authorized health center.
- Valid UAE Health Insurance policy.
- Emirates ID application.
- If mortgaged: Official NOC letter from the financing bank confirming loan status and equity amount.
- If applying jointly with spouse: Attested and MOFA-legalized marriage certificate with Arabic translation.

### Total Government Processing Fees:
Typically between **AED 9,500 and AED 10,500** per applicant, covering DLD registration, medical screening, Emirates ID, and 10-year visa stamping.
